Topic 1: Fundamental and Comprehensive Understanding 
1. What is the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)? Explain in detail its 
importance and why it is used in modern software projects. 
The SDLC is a structured process used to develop software systematically. It 
includes several stages: requirement analysis, design, implementation, testing, 
deployment, and maintenance. Its importance lies in ensuring software quality, 
reducing risks, improving project control, and saving time and cost. In modern 
projects, SDLC supports agile and DevOps methods, helping teams adapt quickly 
to changes and deliver reliable products. 
Use in Modern Software Projects: 
In modern software engineering, SDLC is essential for maintaining control over 
complex development environments. It supports agile, DevOps, and iterative 
models, allowing continuous integration, faster delivery, and better adaptability to 
changing requirements. This makes it a cornerstone of successful software project 
management today.

2. How the Developer’s Role Changes Across SDLC Phases 
SDLC Phase Developer’s Main Tasks 
 Analysis Review requirements and assess technical feasibility. Provide estimates 
and suggestions for implementation. 
 Design Help define system architecture, choose technologies, and design database 
and module structure. 
Development Write clean, efficient, and maintainable code. Integrate modules and 
perform unit testing. 
Testing Fix bugs reported by QA, optimize code, and ensure stability of the system. 
Deployment Support deployment, resolve post-release issues, and ensure system runs as 
expected in production.
